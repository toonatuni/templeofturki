About the Temple

Serving faith, culture, and community with devotion.

Jai Baba Dihbar Temple stands as a spiritual sanctuary where devotees gather for prayer, celebration, and service. We invite everyone to participate in rituals, festivals, and meaningful charitable giving to support temple growth and community care.

Daily Worship

Morning and evening aarti, sacred rituals, and peaceful darshan for every visitor.

Community Service

Food distribution, seva programs, and support for families in need.

Festival Spirit

Grand celebrations with bhajans, pooja, and vibrant community participation.
